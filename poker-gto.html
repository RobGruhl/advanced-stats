What is GTO? Game Theory Optimal play makes mathematically balanced decisions — calling when pot odds justify it, raising with a balanced range, and bluffing at the right frequency. Against perfect opponents, GTO breaks even. Against opponents who deviate, GTO profits from their systematic errors.

The player types:
Regs — Regulars who study the game and grind out a profit. Tough regs are genuinely good and hard to beat.
Recs — Recreational players here for fun. Frequent mistakes, your typical casual Friday night player.
Whales — The biggest losing players. Call way too much and don't care about losing. "Whale watching" = seeking these tables.
Nits — Ultra-tight, only play premium hands. Steal their blinds all day, fold when they bet big.
Calling Stations — Call too often, rarely fold. Hard to bluff, but you print money value betting against them.
Maniacs — Raise and re-raise constantly with junk. Profitable if you're patient, but the variance is wild.

The edge is small but real. Even against average recs, a GTO player might earn only ~8 BB/100 hands — roughly $2/hour at this stake.
